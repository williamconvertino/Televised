# Combat / Weapons Prototype — Usage

This folder implements the combat laboratory described in
[`COMBAT_WEAPONS_PROTOTYPE_README.md`](COMBAT_WEAPONS_PROTOTYPE_README.md). It builds on the shared eyeball player,
surfaces and debug tools in [`../Shared/`](../Shared/README.md) and doesn't change how movement works.

## Getting started

1. Let Unity compile. If `Scenes/CombatSandbox.unity` doesn't exist yet, or was generated by an older layout
   (`CombatSandboxBuilder.LayoutVersion`), it's rebuilt automatically. Unity may first ask to save the scene you have
   open. It needs the movement sandbox's shared assets, which build first.
2. To rebuild it (or if it didn't build), use **Prototyping ▸ Weapons ▸ Rebuild Combat Sandbox**. The rebuild
   regenerates `Prefabs/Enemies/DummyEnemy.prefab` and overwrites the scene.
3. Open it with **Prototyping ▸ Weapons ▸ Open Combat Sandbox** and press Play.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Mouse | Aim (from the player root toward the cursor) |
| Fire button | Fire the current weapon. Default: the mouse button the grapple *isn't* using (LMB). Change it in the WEAPON tab |
| `[` / `]` | Previous / next weapon (the WEAPON tab also has a dropdown) |
| F10 | Toggle the combat panel |
| Tab / F6 | Toggle the movement panel and the movement status box |
| 1–9 | Jump to a spawn point (see below) |
| R, T, C, scroll | Respawn, teleport to the cursor, overview camera, zoom |
| WASD, Space, F1–F9 | Movement as in the movement prototype. F9 toggles the grapple (right mouse) |

When firing uses the grapple's cancel button, that button stops cancelling the grapple. Release the grapple with
Space instead. Clicks on the panels never fire.

## Layout

One 120 × 30 arena, movement first. It combines the shapes and moving objects of both movement sandboxes, and
places enemies in patterns around them. Moving surfaces are orange, static ones blue. From west to east:

| Key | Area | What's there | Spawn groups |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Elevator & Compass (floor) | Elevator beside a ledge, wall overhang, spinner under a stalactite | ElevatorPerch, OverhangBats, SpinnerOrbit, WestFloorLine |
| 2 | Compass centre | Four circles round a point | CompassRing |
| 3 | Big blob | Large irregular blob, tall pillar, circle pairs, a row under the ceiling | BlobOrbit, PillarBruiser, PairGuards, SkyLine |
| 4 | Narrow gap | Two pillars 1.3 apart | GapNest |
| 5 | Windmill tower | Rotating bar round a hub, landing lump, ferry overhead | WindmillRing, LumpWanderers |
| 6 | Orbit core | Stones orbiting a core, pebble cluster | OrbitRing, PebbleNest |
| 7 | Pendulum launch circle | Pendulum from the ceiling | LaunchBruiser, PendulumPerch |
| 8 | Bobbing stones | Alternating static / bobbing stones | StoneHoppers, Seekers |
| 9 | Concave blob | Sliding slab under the ceiling, stretched blob, right wall | SlabRiders, WallStack, Heavy |

**Enemy sizes:** tiny (10 HP, r 0.3), normal (50 HP, r 0.5), large (250–400 HP, r 1.1–1.2).
**Colour shows the movement mode:** dull red = static, orange = patrol, purple = wander, bright red = seek, teal = orbit.
The dark core shrinks as HP drops and turns yellow while skewered.

## HUD

- **Top centre:** the current weapon and its status, with a coloured charge / cooldown bar (green = ready, yellow =
  charging, cyan = active or draining, orange = cooling down, red = overheated), then HP with a health bar.
- **Around the player:** a health bar appears above the eye when you're hurt, or for a moment after any hit. The
  white chunk shows what was just lost. While the weapon isn't ready, the same charge / cooldown bar shows under the
  eye. Toggle both in the DEBUG tab (Player Health / Charge Bars).

## Panel tabs

- **PLAYER**: HP, max HP, *Indestructible Player*, invulnerability window, respawn / restore.
- **ENEMIES**: Respawn All / Respawn Dead / Kill All / Reset All, auto respawn + delay, freeze, global health /
  contact damage / knockback multipliers, movement override (Individual, Force Static / Patrol / Wander / Seek), and
  every spawn group with its own Enabled, Auto Respawn and Movement override.
- **WEAPON**: weapon dropdown, fire button, *Reset this weapon*, then only the selected weapon's settings, grouped
  as damage / weapon-specific / range + falloff / piercing / knockback.
- **IMPACT**: body impact damage, the enemy contact multiplier, self-damage options, impact knockback, and a readout
  of the current approach speed and the last impact.
- **DEBUG**: damage numbers (optionally merging rapid ticks), health labels, detailed hit info, zone labels, projectile
  paths, weapon range, attack geometry, aim assist, knockback vectors, impact velocity; clear projectiles / effects.
- **STATS**: damage dealt / taken, kills, body impacts, self-damage, DPS, and per-weapon uses, hits, damage, kills
  and accuracy (projectile weapons).

Above the tabs: **Reset Entire Sandbox** (player, enemies, projectiles, weapon state, cooldowns, hit bookkeeping,
knockback, respawn timers) and **Save Tuning / Load**. Saving writes every weapon's settings plus the impact, player
and enemy globals to `Settings/CombatTuning.json`. By default that file is loaded when Play starts (DEBUG tab
toggle). Play Mode edits are otherwise lost when you stop, the same as the movement tuning. Custom falloff curves
are edited on the weapon components in the Inspector (`Combat/Weapons`).

## Weapons

| # | Weapon | Geometry | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Eye Beam | lane beam toward the cursor | Charge (0.3 s) → 55 dmg blast. Movement lock optional (off). Terrain splits it |
| 2 | Heavy Shot | projectile | 45 dmg. Gravity arc that ricochets off surfaces and enemies (2 bounces by default, adjustable), big knockback |
| 3 | Burst Shot | projectiles | 4 × 12 dmg, random spread, aim assist that bends shots part-way (can lead moving targets) |
| 4 | Sniper Shot | fast projectile | 60 dmg, 160 u/s (or hitscan), pierces 3, ×0.8 per pierce, no distance falloff |
| 5 | Flamethrower | cone | 70 DPS at point blank, ticks everything inside, strong falloff, heat / overheat |
| 6 | Holy Beam | up + down lane beams | 70 dmg. Charge, locks you in place, surfaces contain / split it. Grants an air jump if fired airborne |
| 7 | Cross Holy Beam | + shape | Same component in Cross mode (Vertical / Horizontal / Cross selectable on both) |
| 8 | Flail | physical circle | Big head on a rope. Click sends it straight at the cursor (no cooldown), hold steers. ~25 dmg at click speed |
| 9 | Chain / Skewer | swept line → rope | Press: skewer. Hold: swing them on a loose chain (slam into walls). Release: fast reel-in and fling |
| 10 | Eye Pulse | expanding circle | 20 dmg + strong knockback: pulse enemies into walls for slam damage |

All numbers are starting points, not balance decisions. The intent is that harder weapons pay off more: beams one-shot
normals, the flail and chain reward movement, and ramming at speed around 12 kills a normal enemy
(`5 + (speed − 3) × 5`).

## Wall slams

Enemies launched into terrain take damage from their speed into the surface, then bounce (SNKRX style):
`base 6 + (speed − 5) × 3`, up to 60, with a 0.25 s cooldown per enemy. Only launched motion counts, never an
enemy's own walking. That means knockback, being flung off the chain, or being swung on it. The damage is credited to
the attack that launched the enemy (STATS shows "+N via wall slams"), and slam numbers are pink with a "!".
Tune it in the IMPACT tab.

## Beams

The eye beam and holy beams are **lane beams**: parallel strips (the Split Resolution slider sets their width), each
raycast on its own. A pillar in front of half the beam stops that half while the rest continues. A strip only exists
if the eye can see its start, so beams spread from the eye and never start behind a wall. Both share one phase model:
charge → active → recovery, each with an optional movement lock, plus *Air jump after firing in the air*. That
option uses `PlayerMotor2D.GrantBonusAirJump`, so it works even with air jumps turned off.

## How damage works

Every weapon hit goes through `DamageEvent.Weapon` → `CombatDamage.Apply`:
`final = base × DistanceFalloff(distance / range) × PierceFalloff(hitIndex) × other`. Knockback is computed from the
same event, and the detailed-hit overlay shows each factor. Projectiles use distance traveled; beams, chains and
cones use distance from the player root; the pulse uses distance from the eye's edge over its radius.
Range fade (`Fade Start Fraction`) is only visual.

Repeat hits are tracked per attack instance (`HitTracker`), so a new beam or chain is never blocked by the last
one's cooldowns.

## Deviations from the design doc

- **Location.** Everything lives in `Weapons/`, where the design doc is, rather than a new `Combat/` folder.
- **No colliders or physics.** Enemies are circles found through `DummyEnemy.All`, and terrain checks use
  `Surface2D` geometry directly, like the movement code. So there are no physics layers to configure, and fast
  shots are swept instead of relying on collision callbacks.
- **Enemies float.** They have no gravity, so they can be placed anywhere. Static enemies drift back to their spawn
  point after knockback, so range tests stay consistent (`Return To Spawn` on the component).
- **The player passes through enemies.** Body impact damages and knocks the enemy back, but the motor has no
  collision response against enemies. Adding one would mean changing movement, which the doc rules out.
- **The chain pulls enemies only.** The optional Pull Split (moving the player) isn't implemented. It works as
  press to skewer, hold to swing on a verlet rope, release to reel in. It doesn't pull as soon as it retracts.
- **Beams are charged, not cooldown-gated.** The eye beam charges and fires rather than being held continuously.
  Holy beams are blocked and split by terrain rather than shining through walls.
- **Enemies have an Orbit movement mode** in addition to the spec's four, for rings circling geometry.
- **Shared code changes, all generic and combat-agnostic:**
  - `PlayerMotor2D.AddMovementLock / RemoveMovementLock` freeze the player without touching tuning. Attached, the
    player still rides moving surfaces. Airborne, it hangs in place. Used by the beams and by death.
  - `PlayerMotor2D.GrantBonusAirJump` gives one extra air jump, lost on landing. Used by the beams' air-jump option.
  - `PrototypeInput.GrappleCancelSuppressed` lets the fire button share the grapple's cancel button.
  - `MovementDebugPanel` exposes `PanelVisible` / `UiScale`, and has an `alwaysShowStatus` option. It's off in this
    scene because the status box blocks clicks in the top-left corner.
- **Extra tools:** a STATS tab, a DPS readout, a heat bar for the flamethrower, a flail whip / steer, and JSON tuning
  save / load.
